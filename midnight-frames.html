Project Description

An exploration of temporal distortion within architectural voids. Midnight Frames investigates how structural permanence reacts to the fluidity of digital light.

The visual language of this project was built around the intersection of brutalist concrete forms and ephemeral light patterns. Shot over a period of three weeks in Berlin's industrial suburbs, the narrative follows no human protagonist, instead treating the architecture itself as the subject of transformation.

Utilizing high-speed cinematography and extreme wide-angle anamorphic glass, we aimed to create a sense of scale that felt both monumental and claustrophobic. The grading was restricted to a deep, textured grayscale to emphasize the grain and texture of the concrete surfaces, stripping away the distraction of color to focus purely on the geometry of the frame.
